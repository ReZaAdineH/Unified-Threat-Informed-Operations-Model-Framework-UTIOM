# UTIOM Lifecycle, Three Pillars and Cross-Cutting Enablers

UTIOM has exactly **three operating pillars**, **seven canonical lifecycle phases** and **three cross-cutting enablers**. These are distinct architectural views and must not be collapsed into one another.

## Three operating pillars

### Leadership & Governance — management and control plane

Leadership & Governance establishes Vision, Strategy, Crown Jewel priorities, governance, accountability, decision authority, investment/resource direction and performance oversight. It receives operational evidence through the strategic feedback loop and adapts the system accordingly.

Primary lifecycle ownership: **Vision, Strategy, Crown Jewels**.

### Engineering & Enablement

Engineering & Enablement turns strategic direction, Crown Jewel context and relevant threats into engineered evidence, telemetry, detection capability and enabling mechanisms.

Primary lifecycle ownership: **Threat Visibility, Threat Detection**.

### Operations & Analysis

Operations & Analysis monitors, analyses, investigates, hunts, decides, contains, responds and converts operational reality into evidence for engineering and governance.

Primary lifecycle ownership: **Response, Continuous Improvement**.

The pillars clarify ownership; they are not independent silos.

## Seven canonical lifecycle phases

1. **Vision** — why Security Operations exists and what success means.
2. **Strategy** — converts Vision into threat-informed priorities and capability direction.
3. **Crown Jewels** — identifies critical services/assets, dependencies, trust boundaries, threat models and realistic attack paths.
4. **Threat Visibility** — engineers and assures the evidence required to observe relevant adversary behaviour.
5. **Threat Detection** — creates versioned, testable, maintainable, threat-informed detection capability.
6. **Response** — prepared analysis, decision authority, containment, response and recovery.
7. **Continuous Improvement** — converts incidents, exercises, metrics and assurance findings into prioritised system change.

**Vision → Strategy → Crown Jewels → Threat Visibility → Threat Detection → Response → Continuous Improvement**

The lifecycle has ordered dependencies but is not a rigid waterfall.

## Three cross-cutting enablers

### Talent

Skills, role design, professional judgement, ownership and learning enable every pillar. Talent has a primary assessment home but cannot be reduced to a single phase.

### Validation

Detection QA, adversary emulation, Purple Teaming, exercises and evidence-based assurance test assumptions across design, engineering and operations. Validation is not a separate lifecycle stage.

### Threat Hunting

Threat Hunting is an operational discipline that uses hypotheses and available evidence to search for activity not already resolved by deterministic detection. It also feeds engineering and threat understanding. It is not a lifecycle phase.

## STRATA

STRATA describes six organisational enabling dimensions:

**Strategy · Talent · Resilience · Automation · Telemetry · Adaptability**

STRATA is a qualitative organisational lens. It does not replace the pillars, lifecycle or cross-cutting enablers and does not independently change UTIOM scores.

## v1.4 assessment tiers

- **Strategic & Governance Foundation**
- **Engineering & Operational Capability**
- **Assurance & Evolution**

The tiers are an assessment/system-integrity view over existing domains. They do **not** replace the three pillars, seven phases or three cross-cutting enablers.

## Feedback loops

An incident-tempo loop connects Engineering & Enablement with Operations & Analysis. A slower strategic decision loop carries operational evidence back to Leadership & Governance.

That feedback is what makes UTIOM an adaptive operating system rather than a linear process.
