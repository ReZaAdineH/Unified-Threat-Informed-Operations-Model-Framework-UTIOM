# UTIOM Licensing and Free/Enterprise Boundary

UTIOM uses a split licensing model so the framework can remain useful and citable while free access does not transfer ownership of the full product or future enterprise implementation.

## Framework/content

The intentionally published UTIOM framework, methodology, Book, diagrams and public framework documentation remain under the applicable public content licence, currently CC BY-SA 4.0 where stated.

Preferred attribution:

> UTIOM — Unified Threat-Informed Operations Model by Reza Adineh — https://utiom.de

## Free assessment use

The assessment workbook and non-software free assessment instruments are available without charge for personal use, internal organisational/business use, education, academic research and practitioner evaluation under the UTIOM Free Assessment Use Terms.

Users own their own assessment answers, organisational data and generated results.

## Software/self-hosting

UTIOM software and self-hosted implementation components identified by the licensing map are provided under **PolyForm Internal Use License 1.0.0**. This supports internal use but does not grant redistribution rights.

## Enterprise/future product

Future enterprise functionality such as APIs, automated evidence collection, continuous assurance, integrations, benchmarking, multi-team workflows, enterprise reporting, access controls and managed services remains proprietary or separately licensed unless expressly released otherwise.

## Historical copies

Previously distributed copies remain governed by the licence under which those copies were originally released. The current licensing boundary does not attempt to revoke earlier grants.
