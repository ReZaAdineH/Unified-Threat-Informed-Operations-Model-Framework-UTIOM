# UTIOM Frequently Asked Questions

## What does UTIOM stand for?

**UTIOM stands for the Unified Threat-Informed Operations Model.** It is a lifecycle framework and operating model for cybersecurity and Security Operations Centers (SOCs), connecting business strategy, threat-informed engineering, Incident Response and Continuous Improvement into one measurable system.

## What are the three core pillars of UTIOM?

The three canonical pillars are:

- **Leadership & Governance**
- **Engineering & Enablement**
- **Operations & Analysis**

They clarify responsibility while preserving one connected operating model.

## What is the UTIOM lifecycle?

UTIOM has exactly seven canonical phases:

**Vision → Strategy → Crown Jewels → Threat Visibility → Threat Detection → Response → Continuous Improvement**

The phases have ordered dependencies but run with iterative engineering and operational feedback loops rather than as a rigid waterfall.

## What problem does UTIOM solve?

UTIOM addresses fragmented Security Operations: strategy disconnected from engineering, tools disconnected from business consequence, telemetry collected without a clear evidence requirement, detection optimised for volume or technique count, response dependent on improvisation, and improvement treated as an occasional project rather than the operating lifecycle.

## Is UTIOM a SOC maturity model?

UTIOM is primarily an operating model, but it includes public maturity, capability, metrics, roadmap and dashboard instruments. Supporting capability maturity models such as TID-CMM and TIR-CMM measure specific parts of the ecosystem in greater depth.

## Is UTIOM a compliance framework?

No. UTIOM does not replace NIST CSF, ISO/IEC 27001, NIS2, DORA, SOC-CMM, MITRE ATT&CK or other established standards and knowledge bases. It helps operationalise and connect relevant requirements and practices.

## How does UTIOM use MITRE ATT&CK?

UTIOM uses ATT&CK as a knowledge base for adversary behaviour. It does not treat all techniques as equally important. Relevant threats, Crown Jewels and attack paths determine which behaviours matter, which telemetry is required, which detections should be engineered and what should be validated.

## Is Incident Response only one phase in UTIOM?

No. UTIOM explicitly treats **Incident Response as the operating mode of Security Operations**. Threat intelligence, modelling, hunting, detection engineering, monitoring, analysis, containment and learning are different expressions of the response mission at different distances from impact. The canonical **Response** phase is where prepared operational action becomes explicit, but response thinking spans the lifecycle.

## What is Telemetry Engineering?

Telemetry Engineering is the deliberate design and maintenance of evidence required to observe relevant adversary behaviour. If an important behaviour must be observable but the required data does not exist, the gap becomes an engineering requirement rather than a permanent blind spot.

## What is Detection QA?

Detection QA validates that detection capability works in the actual environment. It includes logic testing, telemetry validation and adversarial validation. UTIOM treats failed detections as engineering defects that should feed improvement.

## What is the Response Horizon?

The Response Horizon is the UTIOM concept that defensive leverage is generally greater before business impact and that response options become more expensive or constrained as the adversary approaches impact. UTIOM therefore prepares authority, escalation, evidence, playbooks and containment choices in advance.

## Can UTIOM be used to build a SOC from scratch?

Yes. Start at Vision and work forward so purpose, Crown Jewels, threat priorities, visibility, detection, response and measurement are defined before technology drives the architecture.

## Can UTIOM improve an existing SOC?

Yes. Existing internal, outsourced or hybrid Security Operations can use the assessments and roadmap to identify gaps, prioritise improvements, engineer changes, validate them, measure outcomes and reassess.

## Does UTIOM require a specific SIEM, EDR or SOAR platform?

No. UTIOM is vendor-neutral. Technology supports the operating model; it does not define it.

## Are the UTIOM assessment tools free?

Yes. The public framework and tools are released for free use under their stated licence. The current framework is published under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0.

## Is assessment data sent to UTIOM?

The current public browser assessment tools are designed with no assessment backend, no assessment database, no analytics and no signup. Answers remain on the user's browser/device. A self-hosted toolkit and offline workbook are also available.
