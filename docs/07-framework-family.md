# The UTIOM Framework Family and Improvement Paths

UTIOM is the **overarching operating model**. Supporting models and services add measurement depth or operational context without becoming additional UTIOM lifecycle phases and without being averaged into UTIOM scoring.

## UTIOM

**Unified Threat-Informed Operations Model**  
Role: **Security Operations operating model**  
Current framework release: **v1.4**  
Architecture: **3 pillars · 7 lifecycle phases · 3 cross-cutting enablers · 3 assessment tiers**

Website: https://utiom.de

## TID-CMM

**Threat-Informed Detection Capability Maturity Model**  
Role: deeper threat-informed detection and visibility assessment.

A UTIOM roadmap may recommend TID-CMM when Threat Visibility or Threat Detection requires deeper analysis. TID-CMM does not raise the UTIOM score automatically.

Website: https://tid-cmm.com

## TIR-CMM

**Threat-Informed Response Capability Maturity Model**  
Role: deeper response-readiness assessment.

A UTIOM roadmap may recommend TIR-CMM where Response capability, decision authority, containment readiness or response tempo requires deeper analysis. TIR-CMM remains separate from UTIOM scoring.

Website: https://tir-cmm.com

## RSMM

**Realistic SIEM Maturity Model**  
Role: SIEM and monitoring-platform maturity.

RSMM is relevant when UTIOM exposes weaknesses in telemetry/platform engineering, detection support, integration or SIEM operational capability. It should not be triggered merely by weak Validation or Continuous Improvement.

Website: https://rsmm.rezaadineh.com/

## Threat Hunting / Daily Hunt

Role: deeper operational hunting practice and practitioner support.

Threat Hunting is one of UTIOM's three cross-cutting enablers and also has a primary capability-assessment home. Where hunting capability is weak or required, the roadmap may recommend a deeper hunting improvement path. Daily Hunt provides practical threat-informed hunt ideas.

Website: https://hunt.utiom.de/

## KEVMAP

Role: exploitation/exposure context.

KEVMAP is not a maturity model and not a UTIOM lifecycle phase. It supplies exploited-vulnerability and exposure context that can sharpen Strategy, Crown Jewel, Threat Visibility and Threat Detection priorities.

Website: https://kevmap.io

## Relationship rule

**UTIOM decides system direction. Specialist models measure depth. Context services sharpen prioritisation. Improvement services help practitioners act on identified gaps.**

UTIOM remains independently usable. Supporting models constrain or substantiate relevant claims where appropriate; they are not arbitrary weighted inputs that inflate UTIOM maturity.

## STRATA

STRATA is the organisational enabling lens: Strategy, Talent, Resilience, Automation, Telemetry and Adaptability. It supports interpretation and is not an additional lifecycle phase or independent maturity score.

Canonical website: https://utiom.de
