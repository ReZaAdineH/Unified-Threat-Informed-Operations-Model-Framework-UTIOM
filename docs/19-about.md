# About UTIOM

**UTIOM — the Unified Threat-Informed Operations Model — is a Security Operations operating framework created by Reza Adineh.** Its core idea originated **circa 2018** and has been developed into a connected operating model linking management, engineering and operations.

## Why UTIOM exists

Security Operations often accumulates tools, alerts, services and processes faster than it accumulates clarity. Governance, detection engineering, hunting, SOC operations and incident response can become separate disciplines with weak traceability between them.

UTIOM treats Security Operations as a **living product and socio-technical system**. It starts with purpose and business consequence, identifies relevant threats and Crown Jewels, engineers the required evidence and detection capability, prepares response before impact, validates the system and continuously improves it.

## Canonical architecture

- **3 pillars:** Leadership & Governance; Engineering & Enablement; Operations & Analysis.
- **Leadership & Governance:** management and control plane.
- **7 lifecycle phases:** Vision, Strategy, Crown Jewels, Threat Visibility, Threat Detection, Response, Continuous Improvement.
- **3 cross-cutting enablers:** Talent, Validation, Threat Hunting — not phases.
- **STRATA:** Strategy, Talent, Resilience, Automation, Telemetry, Adaptability.
- **3 assessment tiers:** Strategic & Governance Foundation; Engineering & Operational Capability; Assurance & Evolution.
