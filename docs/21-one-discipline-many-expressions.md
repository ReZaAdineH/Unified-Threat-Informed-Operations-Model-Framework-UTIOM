# One Discipline, Many Expressions — Incident Response Across Security Operations

UTIOM treats many activities commonly separated into different Security Operations functions as expressions of the same Incident Response mission at different distances from business impact.

This is why UTIOM says: **Incident Response is not a phase. It is the operating mode of Security Operations.**

## Threat Intelligence

**Incident Response before impact.** Threat Intelligence helps shape Threat Profiling, assumptions, priorities and the adversary behaviours the organisation should prepare to observe and stop.

## Threat Modelling

**Incident Response planning against realistic compromise paths.** Threat models connect relevant adversaries, Crown Jewels, dependencies, trust boundaries and attack paths.

## Detection Engineering

**Incident Response encoded into telemetry and detection logic.** Detection Engineering converts relevant behaviours into evidence requirements and testable detections.

## Threat Hunting

**Incident Response without waiting for an alert.** Hunting uses threat-informed hypotheses to look for relevant adversary behaviour where an alert may be absent or insufficient.

## Monitoring and Alerting

**Continuous low-intensity Incident Response.** Monitoring should focus analyst attention on behaviours and assets that matter rather than treating every alert as equally valuable.

## Alert Triage

**Decision refinement inside Incident Response.** Triage is not merely ticket routing; it develops the context required to decide whether and how the organisation should act.

## Investigation

**Higher-intensity Incident Response.** Investigation establishes what happened, what is affected, what the adversary can do next and which decision is required.

## Containment and Eradication

**Prepared Incident Response execution.** UTIOM prefers predefined authority, playbooks and decision gates over negotiating containment only after impact.

## Forensics

**Evidence, validation and learning.** Forensics supports incident decisions and helps validate whether telemetry, assumptions and controls were sufficient.

## Lessons Learned

**Continuous response feedback.** Lessons are not limited to an optional retrospective; they feed Strategy, engineering, validation and measurement.

## Metrics and Reporting

**Health indicators for the operating system.** Metrics should show whether detection, decision and response capability are becoming faster, more reliable and better aligned to business consequence.

## Training and Exercises

**Incident Response rehearsal.** Exercises validate authority, playbooks, evidence, decision-making and the ability to act inside the Response Horizon.

## Why this matters

When these functions are managed as independent silos, each can optimise locally while the end-to-end response chain remains weak. UTIOM connects them through shared purpose, Crown Jewels, Threat Profiling, attack paths, required evidence, validation and measurable outcomes.
