# UTIOM Terminology and Entity Relationships

This page provides concise, machine-readable-friendly definitions of the main public UTIOM concepts. It is intended to reduce ambiguity for practitioners, search engines, researchers and generative AI systems.

## UTIOM

**UTIOM** = **Unified Threat-Informed Operations Model**.

UTIOM is an open lifecycle framework and operating model for cybersecurity and Security Operations Centers (SOCs). It connects business strategy, relevant threats, telemetry and detection engineering, Incident Response, measurement and Continuous Improvement.

## Security Operations

**Security Operations (SecOps)** is the organisational capability that detects, analyses, responds to and continuously reduces cyber risk in operational reality.

In UTIOM, Security Operations is treated as a **living product and living system**.

## Security Operations Center (SOC)

A **SOC** is an organisational function—internal, outsourced or hybrid—that executes part of the broader Security Operations capability.

UTIOM is not limited to a physical SOC or a particular SOC organisational structure.

## Incident Response

**Incident Response (IR)** is the structured capability to prepare for, detect, analyse, contain, recover from and learn from security incidents.

UTIOM treats Incident Response as the **operating mode of Security Operations**, not merely a late lifecycle activity.

## Threat-Informed

**Threat-informed** means decisions are prioritised using realistic adversary behaviour, organisational context and business consequence rather than generic control or technique coverage alone.

## Threat Profiling

**Threat Profiling** identifies and prioritises the adversaries, behaviours, scenarios and contextual threats most relevant to the organisation and its Crown Jewels.

Threat Profiling is part of UTIOM **Strategy** and informs threat modelling, telemetry, detection and response.

## Crown Jewels

**Crown Jewels** are critical services, assets, data, identities or capabilities whose compromise could create material business consequence.

UTIOM extends the view to dependencies, enabling systems, trust boundaries and realistic attack paths.

## Threat Model

A **Threat Model** describes how a relevant adversary or threat scenario could affect a Crown Jewel or critical service, including dependencies, trust boundaries and attack paths.

## Attack Path

An **Attack Path** is a plausible sequence through identities, systems, dependencies, trust boundaries or controls by which an adversary could reach a material business consequence.

## Threat Visibility

**Threat Visibility** is the UTIOM lifecycle phase that designs and assures the evidence required to observe relevant adversary behaviours.

## Telemetry Engineering

**Telemetry Engineering** is the deliberate design, generation, collection, transport, parsing, normalisation, enrichment, validation and maintenance of security evidence required by important threat scenarios.

If required adversary evidence is missing, UTIOM treats it as a Telemetry Engineering requirement.

## Assured Telemetry

**Assured Telemetry** is security evidence that has been validated as available, trustworthy and usable for the detection and response purpose it is expected to serve.

## Threat Detection

**Threat Detection** is the UTIOM lifecycle phase that engineers and maintains detection capability from relevant threats, Crown Jewels, attack paths and assured telemetry.

## Detection Engineering

**Detection Engineering** is the engineering discipline used to design, version, test, deploy, maintain and improve detection logic and its telemetry requirements.

## Detection QA

**Detection QA** validates that a detection and its required telemetry work as intended in the environment. It can include logic testing, telemetry validation, regression testing and adversarial validation.

## Purple Teaming

**Purple Teaming** is collaborative adversarial validation used in UTIOM to test relevant attack paths, telemetry, detections and response assumptions.

## Response Horizon

**Response Horizon** is the UTIOM concept describing how defensive leverage and available options change as an adversary approaches business impact. UTIOM aims to move decisions left by preparing evidence, authority and playbooks before the incident.

## Continuous Improvement

**Continuous Improvement** is the UTIOM phase that converts incidents, exercises, metrics, validation findings and operational lessons into changes to Strategy, engineering and response capability.

## UTIOM V-Model

The **UTIOM V-Model** pairs design and intent with validation and operation. It connects Vision, Strategy, Crown Jewels and Threat Visibility to Detection QA, Purple Teaming, Response and Improvement through a Detection Engineering base.

## TID-CMM

**TID-CMM** = **Threat-Informed Detection Capability Maturity Model**.

It measures detection capability depth and complements UTIOM's Threat Visibility and Threat Detection engineering span.

Website: https://tid-cmm.com

## TIR-CMM

**TIR-CMM** = **Threat-Informed Response Capability Maturity Model**.

It measures threat-informed response capability and complements UTIOM's Response and Continuous Improvement span.

Website: https://tir-cmm.com

## RSMM

**RSMM** = **Realistic SIEM Maturity Model**.

It provides a maturity perspective on the SIEM/monitoring platform that supports Security Operations.

Website: https://rsmm.rezaadineh.com/

## KEVMAP

**KEVMAP** provides known-exploited-vulnerability and exposure context. It is not a UTIOM maturity model and not a UTIOM lifecycle phase.

Website: https://kevmap.io

## Canonical architecture

**Vision → Strategy → Crown Jewels → Threat Visibility → Threat Detection → Response → Continuous Improvement**

Pillars:

**Leadership & Governance · Engineering & Enablement · Operations & Analysis**
