# UTIOM Community Code of Conduct

The UTIOM community is intended to support serious, constructive and technically useful collaboration around security operations, threat-informed defence, detection engineering, incident response and continuous improvement.

## Expected behaviour

Participants should:

- engage respectfully and professionally;
- challenge ideas with evidence, reasoning and practical experience;
- distinguish fact, observation, assumption and opinion;
- give credit to external work and prior contributions;
- avoid personal attacks, harassment, intimidation and discriminatory behaviour;
- respect confidentiality and never expose customer, employer or production-sensitive information;
- avoid publishing credentials, secrets, private infrastructure details, personal data or sensitive assessment material;
- keep discussion relevant to improving the framework, its implementation guidance, assessments, mappings and public tooling.

## Technical disagreement is welcome

UTIOM is intended to evolve through peer review. Strong disagreement is acceptable and often useful when it is focused on the work rather than the person.

A good challenge explains:

1. what is unclear, incorrect or incomplete;
2. why it matters operationally;
3. what evidence or experience supports the concern;
4. what change or clarification is proposed.

## Canonical framework governance

Community participation does not grant authority to silently redefine UTIOM. The canonical lifecycle, pillars and core doctrine are maintainer-governed. Proposals to change canonical concepts should be explicit, evidence-backed and reviewed openly.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, close or remove contributions that violate these expectations, expose sensitive information, become abusive, or materially disrupt constructive community participation.

Repeated or serious violations may result in participation restrictions.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to UTIOM GitHub issues, pull requests, repository discussions where available, community review activities and other official UTIOM community spaces.

Questions or sensitive concerns should be raised through the official contact path at https://utiom.de rather than through a public issue.
