# UTIOM Public Release History

UTIOM is a living framework. This changelog records public framework releases and material documentation changes.

## v1.4 — September 2026

- Introduced three assessment tiers as a system-integrity/sequencing view over the existing ten capability domains: Strategic & Governance Foundation, Engineering & Operational Capability, and Assurance & Evolution.
- Preserved the assessment content at **50 maturity criteria, 105 capability indicators and 70 metrics**.
- Separated continuous diagnostic capability information from the official ordinal **Governed UTIOM Maturity** result.
- Clarified the existing 50-criteria output as a **Staged Maturity Assessment**, distinct from Governed UTIOM Maturity.
- Restored the complete canonical architecture explicitly: three operating pillars, seven lifecycle phases and three cross-cutting enablers.
- Clarified **Leadership & Governance as the management and control plane** of UTIOM.
- Restored **Talent, Validation and Threat Hunting** as cross-cutting enablers that are not lifecycle phases.
- Clarified STRATA as the organisational enabling lens: Strategy, Talent, Resilience, Automation, Telemetry and Adaptability. STRATA does not independently change UTIOM scores.
- Added gap-driven optional improvement paths to TID-CMM, TIR-CMM, RSMM and Threat Hunting without blending those models into UTIOM scoring.
- Updated the public workbook and self-hosted assessment toolkit to v1.4 while keeping the UTIOM Framework Book at edition v1.2.
- Hardened the public Docker bootstrap to nginx 1.30.4-alpine, image version 1.4, non-root/read-only execution, dropped capabilities and no-new-privileges.
- Standardised the historical UTIOM origin description to **circa 2018**.
- Introduced a split licensing boundary: public framework/content remains under CC BY-SA 4.0; software/self-hosted implementation is provided under PolyForm Internal Use 1.0.0; free non-software assessment instruments use the UTIOM Free Assessment Use Terms; future enterprise functionality remains separately licensed/proprietary.
- Historical copies remain subject to the licence under which those copies were originally distributed.

## v1.3 — August 2026

- Published the canonical UTIOM V-Model and full operating-model architecture.
- Added the public assessment set: 50 staged maturity criteria, 105 capability indicators, 70 metrics, improvement roadmap and capability dashboard.
- Added the v1.3 offline assessment workbook and self-hosting/Docker bootstrap configuration.
- Strengthened the public explanation of the UTIOM ecosystem and the role of TID-CMM, TIR-CMM, RSMM and KEVMAP.

## v1.1 — August 2026

- Published the public assessment instruments for maturity, capability and metrics.
- Added the combined improvement-roadmap workflow and offline/self-hosted assessment options.
- Strengthened validation and adversary emulation as first-class operational capabilities.
- Expanded measurement and automation across applicable lifecycle phases.

## v1.0 — February 2026

- First public edition of the UTIOM framework book.
- Published the full lifecycle and capability-layer model.
- Introduced the threat-informed maturity model and implementation blueprint.

## Repository publication — August 2026

- Established this repository as the public UTIOM framework and community repository.
- Added citation metadata, public security-reporting guidance and contribution rules.
- Explicitly separated public framework material from private production implementation and deployment code.

Canonical website: https://utiom.de
