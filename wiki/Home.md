# UTIOM Ecosystem

The **UTIOM Ecosystem** is a growing family of connected Security Operations models, capability instruments, context services, assessment resources and community mechanisms built around one central idea:

> **Security Operations should operate as one threat-informed system rather than a collection of disconnected functions.**

UTIOM remains the overarching operating model. The ecosystem exists to measure, validate, enrich and operationalise that model without casually changing its canonical architecture.

## The ecosystem at a glance

- **UTIOM** — the operating model for Security Operations.
- **TID-CMM** — detection capability depth: *Would we see the relevant adversary behaviour?*
- **TIR-CMM** — response capability depth: *Could we make the right decision and act in time?*
- **RSMM** — SIEM and monitoring-platform maturity.
- **KEVMAP** — exploited-vulnerability and exposure context for prioritisation.

## UTIOM remains canonical

UTIOM has exactly seven lifecycle phases:

**Vision → Strategy → Crown Jewels → Threat Visibility → Threat Detection → Response → Continuous Improvement**

The ecosystem does not add an eighth phase and does not turn supporting models or services into lifecycle stages.

The three canonical pillars remain:

- **Leadership & Governance**
- **Engineering & Enablement**
- **Operations & Analysis**

## Community model

The public community now has complementary layers:

- **Websites** — canonical public presentation and tools.
- **GitHub repositories** — versioned public knowledge, issues, evidence and accepted changes.
- **GitHub Discussions** — durable public conversation where enabled.
- **Slack** — faster practitioner discussion and working collaboration.

The operating principle is simple:

> **Slack is for conversation. GitHub is where durable knowledge, evidence and accepted framework changes live.**

## The ecosystem is intentionally evolving

The UTIOM Ecosystem is not considered finished. Additional capability models, assurance mechanisms, mappings, research and supporting services may be introduced when they demonstrate a clear role and are sufficiently validated.

Growth does **not** automatically mean more lifecycle phases or more frameworks. A new component should support, measure, validate or enrich the existing operating model rather than redefine it without evidence.

See [Roadmap and Evolution](Roadmap-and-Evolution.md).
